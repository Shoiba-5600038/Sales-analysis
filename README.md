## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing E-commerce and Retail sales data to create structured MIS reports and an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

The objective is to transform raw sales transactions into meaningful business insights related to revenue, profitability, salesperson performance, product categories, and monthly sales trends.

## Project Details

- **Industry:** E-commerce / Retail
- **Project Type:** Sales MIS Reporting & Data Analysis
- **Reporting Period:** January–December 2025
- **Dataset Size:** 5,256 records (reported)
- **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel and Power BI

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare salesperson performance across product categories.
- Track monthly sales trends and changes in performance.
- Evaluate average order value and profit margins.
- Investigate discount patterns and their relationship with profitability.
- Prepare clear, management-friendly MIS reports.
- Present key findings through an interactive dashboard.

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Net Sales:** Total net sales generated.
- **Total Profit:** Total profit across the reporting period.
- **Total Orders:** Count of distinct Order_ID values.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Net Sales divided by distinct order count.
- **Profit Margin (%):** Total Profit divided by Net Sales, multiplied by 100.
- **Total Quantity:** Sum of units sold.
- **Total Discount Amount:** Sum of recorded discount amounts.
- **Monthly Sales Growth (%):** Change in monthly net sales compared with the previous month, expressed as a percentage.

*Note: KPI definitions depend on the dataset's calculation rules. Order counts and order-level measures must be validated before final reporting.*

4. **Product Category Analysis** — Evaluate category-level sales and profitability.

6. **Sales Channel Analysis** — Compare performance across available channels.
7. **Geographic Analysis** — Explore sales by region and city.
8. **Order Status Analysis** — Validate the contribution of different order statuses to reported results.
